Privacy

When you click the toolbar icon, tab titles and URLs (origin and path only — no query parameters) are sent to OpenRouter for grouping. OpenRouter does not log prompts or completions by default. The developer logs are stored locally in this browser and never uploaded; they may contain tab titles and URLs you grouped, plus titles and URLs of all tabs read while selecting candidates.

Developer logs

Runs record what was sent to the model and what it returned. Tab selection records every tab read in the window and which rule excluded it (pinned / already grouped / internal URL / …). Stored locally, capped at ~5 MB (oldest dropped). Never uploaded.
